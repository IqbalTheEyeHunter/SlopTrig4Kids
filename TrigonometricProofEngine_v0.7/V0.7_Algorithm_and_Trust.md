# v0.7 algorithm and proof trust boundary

## Why the first exercise was inconclusive

v0.6.1 admitted F01–F12 (with audited dependencies) but not the angle-addition/double-angle catalogue rules. Its bounded search could not connect sin(2x) with 2 sin(x) cos(x). Larger search limits cannot create a missing justified rule.

The desired short derivation is:

$$\tan x+\cot x=\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}+\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}=\frac{\sin^2x+\cos^2x}{\sin x\cos x}=\frac1{\sin x\cos x}=\frac2{\sin2x}=2\csc2x.$$

The three original restrictions remain: sin(x), cos(x), and sin(2x) are nonzero. The generated report supplies the underlying rotation-coordinate and polynomial certificate for this derivation.

## General algorithm

1. Parse exact expressions. Decimal literals are rational numbers, using the original source spelling (including scientific notation).
2. Preserve the original text's denominator restrictions before cancellation.
3. Recognize rational affine angles in up to four real variables. For each variable, choose a common base using the LCM of its rational coefficient denominators. For x and x/2 this means using x/2 as the base; no half-angle division is needed.
4. Represent each base with cosine and sine coordinates. Derive addition and integer multiples by multiplying rotations; negative angles use the transpose/inverse rotation. Constant shifts in multiples of pi/12 come from pi/4 minus pi/6 and repeated composition.
5. Substitute the quotient/reciprocal definitions of tan, cot, sec and csc. No catalogue identity, target identity, or supplied theorem is an authority for this route.
6. Form exact rational functions and cross-multiply on the retained common domain. Reject identically zero denominators.
7. Reduce the cross-multiplied numerator by the circle relations. Export actual quotient polynomials satisfying N = sum(q_i*g_i), where every g_i is a circle relation, and verify that the remainder is zero.
8. Rebuild the expansions and witness from the claim and compare every certificate field before returning PROVED.

`exercises.py` is only UI/test data. Neither `engine.py` nor `rotation_proof.py` imports it. New coefficients, variables, compound angles and related identities use the same algorithm.

## Foundations and limits of verification

The existing G01 → G03 → G04 checker establishes the unit-circle relation, relative to Euclidean geometry, real arithmetic, area, and unit-circle definitions.

The new geometric foundation `G.ROT` states that oriented Euclidean rotations are linear, their columns are (cos(a), sin(a)) and (-sin(a), cos(a)), and composing rotations adds oriented angles. Multiplying this matrix by the point at angle b yields both addition formulas. This is an explicit trusted geometric interface, not an assumed double-angle identity. `G.SPECIAL` provides the standard exact coordinates of the pi/4 and pi/6 triangles.

The checker executes exact symbolic polynomial arithmetic through SymPy. It does not call trigsimp, expand_trig, Fu transformations or numerical sampling to certify the new route. It is a deterministic replay checker relative to those foundations, not an independently kernel-verified Lean/Coq proof. The proof generator and replay checker share expansion code; tests check generic identities, false variants, certificate mutations, and absence of oracle calls.

Certificates preserve the normalized claim and domain ledger. When checking a certificate against an external original claim, pass that claim's independently computed original domain to `verify_rotation_certificate`; do not treat an untrusted ledger as the original input record. The standalone checker additionally checks the restrictions visible in the normalized exported claim.

## Existing behavior retained

- Direct F08 targets still use the independent geometric report.
- Existing short elementary catalogue routes retain their identity-by-identity reports.
- The target, its dependent catalogue identities, unknown provenance and uncertified supplied results remain excluded by the original admission checks.
- The new certificates can be saved to the theorem bank, replayed, and used for distinct goals. Direct self-use is still blocked.
- `Use supplied identities` does not silently invoke the new rotation backend; it respects the chosen authority restriction.
- Other strategy choices may use the rotation common-form route for compound angles; the report names the actual selected route explicitly.
- Legacy v4 and geometric certificates retain their existing checks. Historical question-bank artifacts are included unchanged and are not relabeled as v0.7-generated proofs.

## Bounded scope

The rotation route supports rational expressions in the six functions, rational affine angles, exact algebraic constants and coefficient parameters. Limits: four angle variables, 48 trig atoms, base denominator 120, integer multiplier 24, input operation count 350, and expanded numerator operation count 15000. These are heuristic resource limits, not a wall-clock guarantee. Symbolic noninteger powers and unsupported angle forms fail closed to the existing bounded search. No square-root sign identity is assumed; sqrt(sin(x)^2) is not replaced with sin(x).

The fallback catalogue search still uses seven identity applications and 260 states. INCONCLUSIVE is possible outside the covered forms and is not a false result. Numerical sampling is only a counterexample diagnostic, as in the prior engine.
