Mathematical trust boundary
---------------------------
The F08 certificate checker validates a fixed G01 dissection schema, exact area
algebra, G03 signed-coordinate distance obligations, G04 unit-circle substitution,
exact goal factorisation, original-domain ledger, dependency list and SHA-256 digest.
The proof is relative to explicit trusted foundations AX.REAL, AX.EUCLID, AX.AREA
and D.UNIT. This release does not claim a set-theoretic formalisation of all
Euclidean geometry.
