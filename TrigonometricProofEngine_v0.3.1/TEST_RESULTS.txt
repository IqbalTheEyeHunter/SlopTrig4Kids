LIMITATIONS
-----------
Proof search is bounded (maximum eight identity substitutions and 140 visited expressions in a direct route).
Conditional theorem assumptions are not yet represented as general logical hypotheses.
Theorem-bank entries and proof certificates are auditable records, not a formal proof-kernel certification.
Domain tracking is conservative and does not solve every trigonometric domain set.
