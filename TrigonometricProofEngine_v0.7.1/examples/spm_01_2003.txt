ALGEBRAIC PROOF

Start with the LHS: tan(x) + cot(x)

1. Use the quotient or reciprocal identity
   = sin(x)/cos(x) + cot(x)
   Reason: tan(x) = sin(x)/cos(x)

2. Use the quotient or reciprocal identity
   = sin(x)/cos(x) + cos(x)/sin(x)
   Reason: cot(x) = cos(x)/sin(x)

3. Combine the fractions
   = (sin(x)**2 + cos(x)**2)/(sin(x)*cos(x))
   Reason: Write the terms over a common denominator.

4. Use the Pythagorean identity
   = 1/(sin(x)*cos(x))
   Reason: cos(u)**2 = 1 - sin(u)**2. Substitute for the squared terms, expand and collect like terms.

5. Simplify the algebra
   = 2/((2*sin(x)*cos(x)))
   Reason: Combine like terms and cancel common factors on the original domain.

6. Use the double-angle identity
   = 2/sin(2*x)
   Reason: sin(2*u) = 2*sin(u)*cos(u), with u = x.

7. Use the quotient or reciprocal identity
   = 2*csc(2*x)
   Reason: csc(2*x) = 1/sin(2*x)

This is the RHS: 2*csc(2*x).
Hence LHS = RHS on the stated domain.
