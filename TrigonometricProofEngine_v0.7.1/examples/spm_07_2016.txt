ALGEBRAIC PROOF

1. Use the Pythagorean identity

   Reason: sec(x)**2 - tan(x)**2 = 1.

2. Use the double-angle identity
   = sin(2*x)/cos(2*x)
   Reason: cos(2*x) = 2*cos(x)**2 - 1.

3. Use the quotient or reciprocal identity
   = tan(2*x)
   Reason: tan(2*x) = sin(2*x)/cos(2*x)

This is the RHS: tan(2*x).
Hence LHS = RHS on the stated domain.
